# CityDesk — production, advertising, and viral plan

CityDesk is a phone-sized world. You open it and you are standing on a real street, anywhere the map has buildings, with the satellite picture of that ground under your feet. Port Harcourt is the first block we tuned. It is not the product boundary.

It is not a coin, not a bank, and not a land registry. The line on the road does not change a title.

## What people do

1. The site opens on the map. There is no brochure page.
2. If you are not signed in, the sign-in sits on the map. You can look around first.
3. One finger turns the view. Two fingers zoom.
4. Buildings are not one grey colour. A church, a shop, a house, and a warehouse each take their own family, and neighbours in the same family still differ.
5. The ground is satellite imagery (Esri World Imagery, the keyless picture [God's Eye View](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view) also boots with). Roads and names stay ours.

## God's Eye View — what we take, what we do not

[bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view) is the live world globe that went to the top of GitHub trending in August 2026. It is a Cesium globe: flights, ships, satellites, quakes, public cameras. It starts with no key on Esri satellite imagery. Google's photoreal 3D tiles are a paid, separate stack.

We do not drop that app into CityDesk.

| Their piece | Here |
| --- | --- |
| Esri satellite ground, no key | Used. It is the ground under our streets. Credit stays on the map. |
| OSM roads and names | Kept. This is how you walk. |
| Google photoreal 3D tiles | Not used. Their terms forbid mixing Google 3D with a non-Google map, and it is metered. Listed as Coming soon until you accept the licence and the bill. |
| Cesium globe, flights, ships, CCTV | Not this product. A different engine, and some feeds are non-commercial. |
| Live "spy satellite" of every building's true face | Not feasible on a phone from public tiles. Satellite ground plus a colour from the building's own type is the honest version. A draped photo-facade for a whole city needs a tile budget. |

Esri can change access. Before a large public launch, read the current ArcGIS World Imagery terms. If they say no, switch the ground to a self-hosted satellite set. Do not scrape Google.

## Coming soon

These are blocked. The map marks each one **Coming soon**. They are not scheduled.

- Photoreal 3D cities (Google / Cesium), licence and cost.
- Offline maps for a whole city.
- Cloud storage for room scans.
- Ada walking beside you.
- Walk to earn. No token.
- Neura laptop sharing. No payouts.
- Ada on the public site.
- A second photo check on the server.
- Generated rooms. Needs a GPU budget.
- Phone AR.

Keys, DNS leftovers, and the `fudcrypt` process are yours to decide. They are not features.

## Production gate

Do not call it launched until all of these are true.

1. You rotate the keys yourself. Agents do not hold them.
2. The public site is a production build, not the development server.
3. Minting stays off. Mainnet stays off. Devnet anchors do not move value.
4. The map opens on a phone (390×844) with satellite ground, distinct buildings, and no page crash.
5. Sign-in works on the map, and a signed-out person can still look.
6. Esri (or the replacement) is attributed.
7. Coming soon stays honest. Nothing in that list is advertised as live.

## Advertising

The ad is the street the viewer already knows.

**Promise.** "Open your city. Stand on your street."

**Do not say.** Earn money for walking. Own the land. A token. A spy feed of people. Guaranteed photoreal buildings.

### Channels

| Channel | What runs | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short video (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) | 8–12 seconds. Drop onto a named place the city knows. Turn once. End on the sign-in sitting on the map. | The recognition is the hook. |
| WhatsApp and Telegram | One place card: name, satellite still, link. No referral code. | This is how a street actually travels. |
| Local creators | One creator per launch city, paid in a normal fee, not a cut of users. They film their own market or church. | A stranger's aerial does not convince. Their voice does. |
| Place partners | A shop, church, or station gets a true name and a "we are here" card. They post it. | The place advertises itself. |
| Press | "A phone map that opens on the satellite of your block." Link the open research, not a token. | One honest paragraph beats a hype thread. |

### City waves

Do not advertise "the whole world" as if every facade is finished. Advertise one city at a time, then the next.

1. Port Harcourt — the tuned block.
2. Lagos — the next recognisable street.
3. One city the team does not live in, to prove it is not a single-town demo.
4. Then a "type any city" cut, with the caveat that untuned cities use satellite ground and typed colours, not hand-made models.

### Media plan, first 6 weeks

| Week | Asset | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 silent turns of real streets, plus one with a voice: "This is our market." | Short video, organic only |
| 2 | Place cards for 10 named spots in the launch city | WhatsApp via the places themselves |
| 3 | One creator film, paid, usage rights in writing | Their channel and ours |
| 4 | A second city, same format | Short video |
| 5 | "Coming soon" said out loud for 3D tiles and the guide. No fake demo. | Caption, not a fake screen |
| 6 | Keep what got forwards. Stop what did not. Do not buy cheap installs. | Review |

### Pipeline

1. Pick the city and five places a local person would recognise.
2. Record on a real phone, on the live site.
3. Cut 9:16, burn in the place name, no music that we do not have rights to.
4. Post organic. Reply in the comments with the link.
5. Ask the place to forward the card. Do not pay per forward.
6. Count: opens of the map, sign-ins, cards forwarded. Not coins.

### What we will not run

Referral ladders, airdrops, pay-to-walk, mining, "limited land mint", scraped faces, or a CCTV wall. Those fight the product and the rules already set.
